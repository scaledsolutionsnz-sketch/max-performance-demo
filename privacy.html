Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how we look after it.

1. What we collect

When you contact us or use the enquiry form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address and details about your vehicle and the work you're after. We only collect what we need to respond to you and carry out the job.

2. How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide a quote
  • Book in and carry out work on your vehicle
  • Contact you about your job or vehicle when needed
  • Keep basic records of work carried out

3. Sharing

We don't sell your information. We only share it where necessary to complete your job — for example, with a parts supplier — or where required by law.

4. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to retain it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

5. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the information we hold about you and request corrections. Get in touch and we'll help.

6. Contact

For any privacy questions, call 027 904 0921 or email info@maxperformance.co.nz.
