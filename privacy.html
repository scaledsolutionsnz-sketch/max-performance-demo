Privacy Policy

Max Performance respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle the information you share with us.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry we collect your name, contact details, vehicle details and the message you send. We only collect what we need to respond to you and quote your job.

How we use it

Your information is used solely to reply to your enquiry, provide quotes and carry out agreed work. We do not sell or trade your personal information to third parties.

Storage

Enquiry details are received by email and kept only as long as needed to deal with your job and any related follow-up. We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you may request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you. Contact us on 027 904 0921 to make a request.
