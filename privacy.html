Privacy Policy

How Max Performance collects, uses and protects your information. This is placeholder content for the demo and should be reviewed before going live.

Last updated:

1. Who we are

Max Performance is a vehicle servicing and performance workshop based in Christchurch, New Zealand. We are committed to protecting your privacy in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

When you contact us or submit a form on this website, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address;
  • Details about your vehicle and the work you're enquiring about;
  • Any feedback or messages you choose to send us.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, provide quotes, carry out and record work on your vehicle, and keep in touch about your service. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Form submissions

Enquiry and feedback forms on this site are delivered to us by a third-party form service (FormSubmit). The information you enter is sent to our email so we can reply. Please avoid sending sensitive information you would not be comfortable sending by email.

5. Sharing

We only share your information where necessary to provide our services (for example, ordering the right parts) or where required by law. We do not pass your details to unrelated third parties for marketing.

6. Keeping your information secure

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and to retain it only for as long as needed for the purposes above.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request a correction. To do so, get in touch using the details below.

8. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Call us on 027 904 0921 or email maxwatson2xtra.co.nz.
